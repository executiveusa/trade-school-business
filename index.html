How it works

Four steps to a launch decision.

Discover

Define the proposed trade, business model, students, location and target start date.

Research

Document Nevada-specific licensing, provider, workforce board and funding requirements using official sources.

Plan

Build a go/no-go brief with cost assumptions, unresolved approvals and immediate next actions.

Coordinate

Help coordinate introductions and preparation; regulatory approvals remain with authorized agencies.

Our purpose

Las Vegas launch focus.

Trade School Business is developing a for-profit consulting service focused on trade-school launch planning in Las Vegas, Nevada. Current services are being validated; this website is a demonstration, not an operating school.

Preview only. Trade School Business is not a licensed school or government funding agency. No grant, license, enrollment or reimbursement is guaranteed. We do not currently collect applications or payments.
